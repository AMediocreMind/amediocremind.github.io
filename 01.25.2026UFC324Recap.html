UFC 324 Recap

The Main Event - Gathje vs Pimblett

In the first UFC event of the year and first on Paramount Plus, the main event did not disappoint and could end 2026 as a potential fight of the year candidate. I guess Paddy “The Baddy” was right when claiming, “I’m a Scouser, we don’t get knocked out,” as he withstood shot after shot from Justin “The Highlight” Gaethje, with the latter dictating a majority of the fight, spending most rounds moving forward, applying the pressure on Pimblett. The fight went the distance in an absolute brawl, with Gaethje becoming the interim lightweight champion by unanimous decision after five action-packed rounds. Gaethje will most likely fight Ilia Topuria next for the UFC lightweight championship belt in a matchup that looks to feature a heavy amount of boxing and stand-up striking.


The Co-Main Event - O'Malley vs Yadong

Song Yadong attempted to chop down the legs of “Suga” Sean O’Malley in a two vs. five-ranked bantamweight co-main event at UFC 324 in Las Vegas late Saturday night. O’Malley proved resilient to the damage, showing a clear speed advantage, closing with a damage-inflicting third round to win in a 29–28 unanimous decision. With the win, O’Malley ends a two-fight losing streak and sets the stage for an interesting battle for the bantamweight belt, with Merab Dvalishvili potentially in line for a rematch with Petr Yan to regain his title and O’Malley waiting in the wings to fight the winner.


Full Results

  • Justin Gaethje defeated Paddy Pimblett, Unanimous Decision
  • Sean O’Malley defeated Song Yadong, Unanimous Decision
  • Waldo Cortes-Acosta defeated Derrick Lewis, KO/TKO
  • Natalia Silva defeated Rose Namajunas, Unanimous Decision
  • Jean Silva defeated Arnold Allen, Unanimous Decision
  • Umar Nurmagomedov defeated Deiveson Figueiredo, Unanimous Decision
  • Ateba Gautier defeated Andrey Pulyaev, Unanimous Decision
  • Nikita Krylov defeated Modestas Bukauskas, KO/TKO
  • Alex Perez defeated Charles Johnson, KO/TKO
  • Josh Hokit defeated Denzel Freeman, KO/TKO
  • Ty Miller defeated Adam Fugitt, KO/TKO
