UFC 325 Preview

Setting The Stage

The second of back-to-back numbered UFC events takes place this upcoming weekend as the UFC heads “Down Undah” for its second event of the year. It all takes place this weekend at the Qudos Bank Arena in Sydney Olympic Park, New South Wales, Australia. Fights are set to start on Saturday, January 31st, at 5 PM Eastern. As expected, we’ll have a heavy dose of Aussie and New Zealand native fighters across the entire card.


Early Prelims

The early prelims start us off with flyweights Aaron Tau versus Namsrai Batbayar. The 32-year-old Tau has an 11-1 professional record, with seven victories by TKO/KO, and is riding a three-fight win streak. His opponent, Batbayar, is looking for his sixth win in a row, and the 25-year-old is 9-1 as a professional, including four wins by KO/TKO.


The 10-3, 20-year-old Sulangrangbo will take on the 7-1, Lawrence Lui in a bantamweight bout as our second fight of the evening. Both fighters have finished over half of their professional fights by way of KO/TKO.


Keiichiro Nakamura and Sebastian Szalay will fight next in the featherweight division. The former is 7-1 with six wins by knockout, while Szalay sports a 10-1 professional record, outlasting most of his prior opponents and winning seven times by decision.


The following fight features a clash of unique nicknames in the lightweight division as “Frog Man,” Sang Uk Kim and “Street Buddha,” Dom Mar Fan face off. The 32-year-old Sang Uk Kim is 13-3 professionally, while Dom Mar Fan is 25 years old and has an 8-2 record. Both are on four-fight win streaks.


The last of our early prelims features a featherweight battle between two submission artists, Kaan Ofli and Yizha. The experienced 29-year-old Yizha is 26-5 professionally, with fourteen wins by way of submission, while the 32-year-old Kaan Ofli is 12-4-1, with six submission wins.


Prelims

The up-and-comer, sporting one of the best nicknames on the card, Oban Elliott, “The Welsh Gangster,” takes on Jonathan Micalleff in the first fight of the prelims portion of the event. Elliott was on an eight-fight win streak before losing to Seok Hyeon Ko back in June of last year, by unanimous decision. Micalleff is on a three-fight win streak after a unanimous decision victory against Kevin Jousset almost a year ago. Originally, this welterweight matchup was scheduled for September of last year, but was canceled due to illness on the part of Elliott.


Jacob Malkoun and the undefeated Torrez Finney will fight next in a middleweight matchup, as Malkoun, who is 8-3 professionally, will look to hand Finney his first professional loss. Finney, who is 11-0, has won seven fights by knockout. Malkoun is coming off a win, but has not fought since March 30, 2024.


The 6-foot-3-inch middleweight Cam Rowston will look to continue his five-fight win streak as he takes on Cody Brundage, who is coming off a split decision loss to Eric McConico back in August. Both fighters have shown versatility in finishing fights, with the 11-7-1 Brundage having won six fights by knockout and three by submission. His opponent Rowston has finished seven of his thirteen victories by submission and five by TKO/KO.


The last fight of the prelims is a light heavyweight bout between the Australian, Junior Tafa, and the American, Billy Elekana. Tafa has seen mixed results in his professional MMA career, with a 6-4 record and all of his victories coming by way of knockout. Coming off a loss due to an arm triangle choke back in June, Tafa will look to get back in the win column against Elekana, who is 9-2 professionally and has won his last two fights.


Main Card

With Jamie Mullarkey stepping in as a replacement for Rhongzu, the matchup with Quillan Salkilld is the only Aussie versus Aussie matchup on the entire card. The 26-year-old Salkilld is 10-1 in his professional fighting career, having only lost his first professional fight. He will look to keep the streak going and continue his rise in the UFC’s lightweight division against Mullarkey, five years his senior. Mullarkey is coming off a win, but has had mixed results recently, losing three of his last five fights.


Tai Tuivasa might have the streakiest fight history of any fighter in the UFC. The number-twelve-ranked heavyweight has lost five fights in a row after riding a five-fight win streak. Before that, he had lost three fights in a row, following a five-fight win streak. He will look to start a new winning streak against the 26-year-old, Tallison Teixeira. Teixeira lost last July to Derrick Lewis, but had previously won eight fights in a row.


Before losing his last fight to Benoît Saint Denis, the fourteen-ranked Mauricio Ruffy had strung together seven straight wins, with all but one ending by way of KO/TKO. The Brazilian faces Rafael Fiziev, the ninth-ranked lightweight contender. Fiziev is coming off a win in his most recent fight, but had previously lost three fights in a row, including two to the interim lightweight champion, Justin Gaethje, and one to Mateusz Gamrot.


Benoît Saint Denis and Dan Hooker, aka Dan “The Hangman” Hooker (best nickname on the card, maybe in the entire UFC), will square off in a ranked lightweight matchup (Hooker #6 / Saint Denis #8). The 35-year-old UFC veteran Hooker is 24-13 and looking to get back in the win column after losing to Arman Tsarukyan back in November. Prior to this loss, he had won three straight fights. Saint Denis was one of the biggest risers in the UFC, winning five straight fights before back-to-back losses to Dustin Poirier and Renato Moicano, but has regained momentum winning his last three fights, all of which ended in finishes.


The main event features a featherweight title fight and a rematch from April of last year, as the champion, Alexander Volkanovski, takes on the number-two-ranked Diego Lopes. Last time these two met, Volkanovski got the better of Lopes, earning a much-needed five-round unanimous decision victory, following back-to-back losses to Ilia Topuria and Islam Makhachev. The 37-year-old will look to retain the title against the 31-year-old Lopes, who is looking to avenge his lone loss in his past seven professional fights and is coming off a win against Jean Silva back in September.
